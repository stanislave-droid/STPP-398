<h3>About the website:</h3>

<p>Website has a unique style and beautiful animations,
  for better UX, the header was made fixed to the top.
  The website has 6 main sections, a header, and a footer,
  as well as 2 additional pages for privacy policy and terms of service.
  To make the website similar to the atmosphere of the game, we added animations:
  show animations and basic animations for objects, some of which are presented here, below this text.
  Also the header detects where you are on the page and displays it by switching lines under the navigation links.
</p>
