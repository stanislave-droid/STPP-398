Core Gameplay

Match, build, and explore

  • Solve Colorful Match-3 Levels

    Complete unique objectives to earn valuable rewards.

  • Restore Island Buildings

    Renovate cottages, cafés, docks, and scenic landmarks.

  • Unlock New Areas

    Explore hidden beaches, tropical forests, waterfalls, and mountain viewpoints.

  • Collect Useful Boosters

    Use powerful combinations to overcome challenging puzzles.

  • Personalize Your Paradise

    Decorate outdoor spaces with flowers, pathways, and island ornaments.
